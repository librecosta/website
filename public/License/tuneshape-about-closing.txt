TuneShape Soft Blur In and Hover Preview
Sources: soft-blur-in-prompt-project.zip and hover-preview-prompt-project.zip,
both src/motion.js. Soft Blur In includes TuneShape's browser adaptation of
Remocn's effect (https://remocn.dev/docs/typography/soft-blur-in).

Closing preview images:
- /about/closing/works-world.webp: LibreCosta /works, eight-island overview
  captured in Chromium and downsampled. See /works/asset-notices.txt for
  the depicted world's asset credits.
- /about/gopheratlas-og.png: existing official GopherAtlas Open Graph image.
- /about/closing/librecosta-github.webp: LibreCosta's stacked charcoal-on-white
  lockup from the supplied brand kit, resized and encoded as WebP.
