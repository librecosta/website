LibreCosta WORKS assets

The single-island camera framing and model fitting adapt code from Wenz Louie
Rejas's Interactive 3D Portfolio, commit 0319381ab0310cdabaca734d4e9c92bfcee79a49.
Source: https://github.com/wenzrejas/wenzrejas.github.io
Copyright (c) 2026 Wenz Louie Rejas. See wenz-license.txt (MIT).

Orbit Reel geometry and retargeting adapt the TuneShape Orbit Reel export.
Copyright (c) 2026 TuneShape contributors. See ../License/tuneshape-orbit-reel.txt (MIT).

Three.js and the Motion packages used by WORKS are listed in runtime-licenses.txt (MIT).
The Draco glTF WASM decoder and JavaScript wrapper match the Three.js r187
distribution. See draco-license.txt (Apache 2.0).

All eight original WORKS models are licensed under BSD-3-Clause, including
their editable Blender model files, GLB exports, and included materials and
textures. The license text is reproduced below. For the full repository scope,
see https://github.com/librecosta/website/blob/master/ASSETS-LICENSE.md.

islands/harvest.glb (891,716 bytes) — The Collector's Workshop. Original LibreCosta
Blender model, surface atlases and baked contact/daylight shading. The editable
source and export scripts are in assets/works/harvest/ in the website repository.

islands/liquid-glass.glb (1,081,908 bytes) — The Refraction Atelier. Original LibreCosta
Blender model, opaque surface atlases and baked contact/daylight shading, with separate
PBR transmission and thickness for the optical pieces. The editable source and export
scripts are in assets/works/liquid-glass/ in the website repository.

islands/sofaraway.glb (1,111,248 bytes) — The Beatline. Original LibreCosta
Blender model, opaque surface atlases and baked contact/daylight shading, with
independent layered flight glazing, control window transmission and emissive
rhythm lights. Editable source and export scripts are in assets/works/sofaraway/
in the website repository.

islands/gopher-atlas.glb (1,873,704 bytes) — The Atlas Observatory. Original
LibreCosta Blender model, packed PBR atlases and baked contact/daylight shading,
with independent PBR glazing and warm lights. Editable source and export
scripts are in assets/works/gopher-atlas/ in the website repository.

islands/tool4furry.glb (1,831,560 bytes) — The Makers' Studio. Original LibreCosta
Blender model, packed PBR surface atlases, baked contact/daylight shading, and
separate clear and diffuse glazing and warm lights. Editable source and export
script: assets/works/tool4furry/.

islands/wavefield.glb (933,268 bytes) — The Wave Loom. Original LibreCosta Blender
geometry, PBR surface atlases and baked contact/daylight shading, with independent
silver-blue fibres, translucent gossamer and workshop windows. Editable source
and export script: assets/works/wavefield/.

islands/gofurry.glb (1,826,616 bytes) — The Discovery Harbor. Original LibreCosta
Blender geometry with packed PBR surface atlases, baked contact and daylight
shading, independent glazing and warm lights. Editable source and export script:
assets/works/gofurry/.

islands/uptime.glb (1,459,392 bytes) — The Vigil Station. Original LibreCosta
Blender geometry, packed PBR atlases and baked contact, daylight and interior
shading, with independent glazing and amber lights. Editable source and export
script: assets/works/uptime/.

The original island renders, reel placeholder glyphs and Harvest dossier artwork
are copyright (c) 2026 Chengdu Yuanan Kaiwu Technology Co., Ltd. All rights
reserved in this distribution. Earlier grants continue under their terms.

Project previews

The GoFurry, Tool4Furry, LibreCosta Uptime, GopherAtlas and SoFarAway dossiers use
the shared images documented in ../about/asset-notices.txt.

previews/*.webp: LibreCosta renders of the eight original islands listed above.
All eight use the single-island viewer's orthographic camera settings, authored
model bearings, materials and lighting. Captured with transparency on an
800 × 600 CSS-pixel stage, then resized to 560 × 420 and encoded as WebP.
previews/harvest.webp (44,148 bytes) was recaptured after the Blender export using
the WORKS single-island renderer, including Harvest's authored fill light.
previews/liquid-glass.webp (46,278 bytes) was captured from the same viewer after L2
integration, with the atlas shading, PBR transmission and atelier reflection lighting.
previews/sofaraway.webp (60,904 bytes) was captured from the same viewer after S2
integration, with baked shading, layered glazing and static rhythm lights.
previews/gopheratlas.webp (34,898 bytes) was captured from the same viewer after
G2 integration, with baked stone, slate and timber and inset glazing.
previews/tool4furry.webp (48,776 bytes) was captured from the same viewer after
T2 integration, with the warm timber interior, clear workshop windows and
translucent clerestory glazing.
previews/wavefield.webp (41,916 bytes) and wavefield-preview.webp (65,342 bytes)
were captured from the same WORKS single-island viewer at 4:3 and 16:9 for the
reel and dossier respectively. Both show LibreCosta's Wave Loom island.
previews/gofurry.webp (37,066 bytes) was captured from the same viewer after GF2,
with the terracotta vault, glazed gallery and curved harbor walk.
previews/librecosta-uptime.webp (36,682 bytes) was captured from the same viewer
after U2, with the octagonal lantern room and stepped approach.
