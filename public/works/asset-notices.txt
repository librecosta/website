LibreCosta WORKS assets

terrain.webp: original LibreCosta terrain, 106,466 bytes. The source generator
is scripts/render-works-terrain.py in the website repository. No external
terrain, map data, or imagery is incorporated.

GoFurry: logo-mini.svg from apps/web/src/assets in the GoFurry workspace.
Sofaraway: shots/play.png from the LibreCosta project workspace, revision 5ca93c6.
Wavefield: https://github.com/librecosta/wavefield/blob/main/docs/assets/wavefield-preview.webp
Tool4Furry: public/brand/tool4furry-og.png from the Tool4Furry project workspace.
tool4furry-mark.webp (1,044 bytes) isolates the mark from the same source.
LibreCosta Uptime: https://github.com/librecosta/uptime/blob/master/docs/assets/uptime-dashboard.webp
Liquid Glass: https://github.com/librecosta/liquid-glass/blob/main/docs/playground.webp
GopherAtlas: https://gopheratlas.com/images/og-default.png (official publication artwork).
