Adapted as the buried left ruin. The tallest antenna was trimmed.

Centered, rescaled and given the scene's warm mineral material. The runtime
GLB omits original maps and unused vertex data, welds matching vertices, and
packs the mesh attributes and indices. It is 645 kB with 32,399 triangles.
See scripts/pack-future-city.py for the geometry export.
