Both were centered, rescaled and given the scene's warm mineral material. The
runtime GLB omits original maps and unused vertex data, welds matching vertices,
and packs the mesh attributes and indices. The two supplied GLBs total 6.04 MB.
The runtime file is 1.32 MB with 55,439 triangles. The original files retain
their source data. See scripts/pack-future-city.py for the geometry export.
